AgentDomain is the agent-native identity and communications infrastructure.

Developers can integrate directly through APIs and SDKs, people can guide agents, and autonomous
workflows can operate within configured permissions. Domains, DNS, HTTPS, email, optional onchain
names, and AgentID support that foundation. Registration is quoted and paid in USDC on Base
through x402.
